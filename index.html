FAQ

Questions & answers

Do I need any medical background?

No. First Aid and CPR & AED are made for everyday people. If you can follow clear steps, you can learn this. BLS is the course designed for healthcare providers.

Which course is right for me?

Parents, teachers, staff and shul members usually start with First Aid and/or CPR & AED. Nurses, EMTs and other healthcare workers usually need BLS. Not sure? Send us a message and we'll help you choose.

Are classes separate for men and women?

Yes. We have male and female instructors, and men's and women's classes are held separately.

What language are classes taught in?

Yiddish or English, whichever your group prefers.

Can you come to our shul, school or office?

Yes. Classes can be held in your shul, school, office or home, for groups or for individuals.

Will I be certified?

Yes, these are certified courses. When you book, we'll go over the certification that comes with the course you choose.

How much does it cost, and how long is a class?

It depends on the course and the size of your group. Contact us for schedule & pricing.
